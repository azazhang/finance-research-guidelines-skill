# Lessons from Research Execution

The failure was pervasive across research selection, execution, inference, interpretation, writing and reporting. Work that was easy to verify displaced the economic comparison that would establish a contribution. The economic question disappeared; constructs and sample eligibility were mishandled; reproducibility was called validity; identification lost priority; literature roles were misunderstood; outputs were reported as findings; and governance recreated the same tinkering. These lessons govern the next substantive action, not another self-assessment or documentation cycle.

| Failure and cause | Productive replacement |
|---|---|
| Resuming from the latest parser or source issue erased the paper's economic question. | Recover the argument and first unfinished task from the existing state, then execute it. |
| Source reads, adjudication, inventories, provenance, and repeatedly dispatched reviewers offered small visible completions. | Use completed data and measures for the intended economic comparison; delegate only distinct permitted outputs. |
| “Necessary dependency” became a way to reopen completed stages. | Require a demonstrated defect or user-authorized extension, with a bounded correction; relevance alone does not authorize a detour. |
| Plans and increasingly elaborate controls recorded busyness instead of governing choices. | Judge progress by the assigned scientific deliverable. Follow the ordered plan; do not substitute new plans, gates, or certifications. |
| Measurement development continued indefinitely and reproducibility was called validity. | Define the construct, use all compatible observations, and target independent financial benchmarks to the interpretation claimed. Reuse the resulting measure. |
| Literature was read as superficial precedent, conflating auxiliary model use with measurement. | Establish the cited paper's actual argument, construct, design, evidence, and current publication status before positioning the study. |
| Legitimate exploratory specifications were mistakenly restricted while edge work expanded. | Run focused comparisons efficiently within the assigned task, preserve baseline and material variants, and retain uncertainty and selection decisions. |
| Every attempted regression was called a finding or inserted into the paper. | Preserve exploration in the research record. Select paper evidence for its economic role and disclose adverse evidence that changes a retained claim. |
| Argument-making was deferred while data, estimates and qualifications accumulated; falsifiability was treated as a demand for certainty. | Advance an economic answer: what the reader should believe, why, which comparison supports it, and what it changes relative to the literature. Calibrate its scope to evidence; do not erase the claim. |
| The author acted as a referee, repeatedly qualifying instead of explaining the evidence. | Make a direct, calibrated economic argument; give a material limitation one primary home. |
| A numeric abstract and temporary word substitutions were mistaken for scholarly writing. | Write a connected explanation of tension, question, design, main evidence, and meaning; maintain finance register after every later revision. |
| A clearer alternative draft was accepted without checking cohort and inferential meaning. | Preserve history, adopt better exposition, and reconcile claims to current estimates without drifting into unsupported intent or causation. |
| User reports recited files, diagnostics, and compliance claims. | Give a self-contained academic explanation of the comparison, magnitudes, uncertainty, interpretation, and actual manuscript change. |

Physical organization was also overstated: a new entry point left legacy top-level folders and an obsolete-looking dated state file in place. At an assigned organization checkpoint, group related assets physically, separate current status from history, archive superseded state and repair dependent paths. More documentation over the same clutter is not the requested deliverable.

An organized workspace or a compiled paper can support research. They are the deliverable when explicitly assigned, as at a phase checkpoint; they are not substitutes for empirical findings during substantive analysis. Similarly, provisional phase completion is an administrative and scientific status statement, not certification of an A* contribution.

No skill can guarantee adherence. The practical discipline is to stop selecting the known detour and perform the next authorized substantive action. More instructions are warranted only when they resolve a demonstrated ambiguity or change that choice.
